# Bundled fonts

All fonts in this folder are open-license (SIL Open Font License 1.1 or compatible) and come from the Google Fonts
repository (https://github.com/google/fonts, ofl/ directory). Each family's license text is available in that repo
next to the font files. Fonts not bundled here are downloaded on demand (see core/font_files.py) or replaced by a
system font, and every substitution is recorded in the plan's fallback log.
